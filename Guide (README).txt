This is a test version of all the innovations in the game's combat system; therefore, only some characters that have already been reworked will be available.

An Awake Bar has been implemented that is fixed below the character. To Transform or execute the Ultimate, you need it full. (Characters without a transformation receive a temporary status buff.)

Characters can have stable or temporary transformations:

Stable Transformation: resets your Awake Bar and maintains your form; you can return to your base form when the bar is full again and press down+X.

Temporary Transformation: refills the Awake Bar and maintains the transformation as long as the bar lasts, and you can perform the Ultimate at any time while transformed.

New Commands:

The traditional down, forward, A has been removed. The new mechanic only requires holding the charge "S" + any attack. With this, the character now has 4 skills instead of 6, and some vary their skills when activating the Awake state.

Skill 1: S+A -1 Power level
Skill 2: S+B - 1.5 Power level
Skill 3: S+C - 1.5 Power level
Super: S+Y - 2 Power level

Dodge & Parry System: Now every character has the option to dodge and parry using the Y button.

Y: Dodge
->+Y: Forward Dodge
->+Y: Backward Dodge
Down+Y: Parry

Good luck and please give feedback in our Discord community :)
